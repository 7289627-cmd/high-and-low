# High & Low: handoff (Nov 17, 2026)

For any person or AI session picking this project up. All paths are relative to the root of the GitHub repo `7289627-cmd/high-and-low`, branch `main`. Nothing secret is written here.

## 1. Goal
High & Low is a phone party number-guessing game: everyone hides a number, you ask "higher or lower" questions, and guess theirs before they guess yours. The owner (Dodgy) wants it live in the App Store and Google Play, then its first 100 players. Launch is outside the EU for now. It earns from ads plus a one-time Remove Ads purchase, which come in an update after the first test build.

## 4. Decisions
Approved by the owner, do not change:
- Very simple UI: no extra labels, hints or tutorials, no emoji in UI chrome. Real recorded sounds only. No HALF button.
- No login. Coins only from winning. Hidden fill-in bots are allowed and disclosed in the "i" sheet.
- No leaderboard. Ratings stay on the phone.
- First test build has no ads. Ads and Remove Ads come in an update.
- Non-EU launch. EU later, possibly as a no-ads version or under a business.
- Declared: 13 and up, frequent cartoon violence, contains ads, belching humour, players interact, no chat.
- Support email is the Liquid Horizon one for now. A game domain is a later option.
- Small visual changes: quick check, push, long test run afterwards.

Proposed, not approved (do not build or enable):
- Monthly deletion of play statistics older than 14 months (the privacy page promises it, no job exists yet).
- Rewarded ads. The interstitial frequency rule. Streak and share features. Guest accounts. iPad support. Paid testers.
Rejected: preview video as a store asset, synthesized sounds, slide whistle.
