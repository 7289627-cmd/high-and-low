## Large text
All layout is px, so changing `html{font-size}` changes nothing (identical at 125% and 150%, no breakage; but the app also ignores such a setting). I also simulated an OS text zoom by scaling every element's font-size. 125%: no breakage on home or ask slider at 390 or 320. 150%: logo clipped (home 390), mode labels "1 – 10,000" overflow tiles, HIGHER chip overflows by 4px at 390, ask slider crowded and page 53px taller at 320. NOT fixed (would need layout work or a text-size decision). Screenshots in test/shots/a11_*.
