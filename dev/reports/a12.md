## Facts learned (code reading, then confirmed by test)
- Peers are swept after 10 s of silence on both sides. A silent joiner keeps its seat until 90 s (host side). A joiner whose page keeps running gives up on the host at 60 s, so in practice it leaves itself before the host replaces it. "Replaced by a bot" only meets a returning human when the phone was frozen (iOS) for over 90 s. New hook `HL_TEST_GONE_MS` (default 90000) makes the 90 s testable.

## Scenarios: today's behaviour -> change
(a) joiner silent 8 s / 14 s, friends room: resyncs, same seat, same secret (secret is reloaded from localStorage after G was null). OK, no change. The game state comes back with the host's next 3 s heartbeat.
(b1) joiner replaced by hidden easy bot, page alive, returns: BUG, dead screen (own card, no controls, no hint, only Leave). Fix: `replaced()` in main.js. Such a phone now shows the existing "You're watching, and you'll be in the next one" view. toLobby already drops hb bots, so the person re-enters next round.
   Also BUG: the bot keeps the human pid, so if it won, that phone got coins (countWin), "You win!", a winner sheet and a rating WIN in Play. Now: no coins, no sheet, tray says "Game over", rated duel counts as a loss (abandoned).

(c) host silent 8 s: no duplicate or skipped turns (turnNo monotonic, one turn per turnNo), nobody replaced, game finishes. OK, no change.
(d1) rated duel, joiner vanishes for good: host replaces it with a bot, finishes, rating applied exactly once (hl-elo-n +1, not re-applied by later ticks or by leaving). OK.
(d2) rated duel, host vanishes: joiner cannot finish (game lives on the host phone). After the 10 s sweep eloAtStake is 0, it goes home at host-lost time with NO rating change. Intended, asserted.
(d3) BUG (unfair loss): opponent gone 10..90 s (not replaced yet), host taps Leave: stake dialog and a real loss. After takeover the opponent is a bot and leaving still costs rating (normal Play rule, unchanged).
