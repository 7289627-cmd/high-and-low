## Behaviour
- Win streak: rated Play duel (pub, 2 players) win +1, loss or mid-game leave resets. Badge (flame + number) at 2 or more, home beside rating pill and result sheet.
- Day streak: a day counts only when a duel is played to the end (a quit does not mark the day). 7 dots on home only (green = played). Streak stays alive on the day after the last play until that day ends.
- Double coins: first coin win per local day pays 10, rest 5. Still only from winning.
- Flag off: no storage writes, helpers inert, DOM identical (tested).

## Owner decisions: pros/cons of shipping
- Pros: gives a reason to open the app daily with almost no UI; double coin is a pure bonus, not a penalty; dots are passive and never nag; flag lets you try it safely.
- Cons: streak loss frustration (one loss kills a 5-streak, and the flame vanishes); rating and streak both reset on quit, which punishes rage-quitting twice (arguably the desired effect, but harsh on dropped connections and app backgrounding); the dots are unlabeled, so new players may not understand them; day boundary is device-local so travel/clock changes can break or cheat streaks; the data is local only (new phone or cleared storage loses it); first win doubling is only +5, so it is a weak lure; no push reminders, so the loop depends on memory.
- Decide: ship on or off; whether a quit should mark the day; whether the win-streak should have a grace (e.g. forgive a disconnect).
