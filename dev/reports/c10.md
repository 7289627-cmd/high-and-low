# c10: phone-app feel (haptics, Back button, lifecycle, wake lock)
Patch: out/c10.patch (real changes only; path-only edits to tests/build paths in the sandbox copy are left out except the one build.py line).
## Changed
- src/haptics.js (new, in build.py right after music.js): `haptic(kind)` for tap/tick/thud/win/lose. App: `HLNative.haptic(kind)`. Web: `navigator.vibrate(pattern)`. Else no-op, never throws. Only `tick` is rate limited (40 ms). No setting, no mute link. `hapticImpactAt(kind)` returns the first impact cue time from `DEATH_SFX[kind]` (first cue whose name is an impact sound such as k_clang, k_slam, k_crack, k_explode, k_popbang, d_piano, d_slam, k_eat1, k_gloop2, bonk; skips squeaks, fuses, k_boom; else first cue; else 0.9 s). DEATH_SFX untouched. Impact times: anvil 1.36, zap 1.5, rocket 2.3, chomp 1.42, melt 1.6, pop 2.05, piano 1.42, banana 2.1, bees .45, calc 2.05. This is my reading of "impact cue", say if you want other moments.
- src/main.js wiring (one line each): slider detent in `sliderBind` and `secBind` (only inside the existing "value changed" branch), `lockSecret` (tap), your turn (tap, next to sfx yourturn), `deathSfx` (thud via `later`, so `clearTimers` cancels it), `showWinner` (win or lose).
- Back: `hlBack()` (global `window.hlBack`) added after the DEEP block in main.js. Open sheet: clicks `#stayBtn` (rated warning), else `#ovX`, else `#ovClose`; wheel and death scenes are swallowed (they always play in full). In a game: clicks `#leaveBtn` (existing flow: rated warning sheet, or "Tap to leave" arming, second Back leaves). Home: calls `HLNative.exit()` if present, returns false. Web only (no HLNative): one sentinel history entry pushed at load and re-pushed after popstate and on pointerup (Chrome skips entries made without a tap); Back on home calls `history.back()` to really leave. `#room=` is already stripped by the existing replaceState before the sentinel is pushed; `#flags=` and `#player=` are kept (pushState uses `location.href`).
- app/native/hlnative.js: `haptic` accepts tap/tick/thud/win/lose (LIGHT, selectionChanged, HEAVY, SUCCESS, ERROR); new `HLNative.exit()` = `App.exitApp()`; `App` `backButton` listener calls `window.hlBack()` (falls back to exit). app/README.md: two bullets. `@capacitor/haptics` 8.0.2 and `@capacitor/app` 8.1.2 were already pinned in app/package.json and imported in entry.js, so no new dependency and no new README step beyond `npm install` and `npm run sync`.
- Lifecycle: music engine already handles it (a08, music.js: on `visibilitychange` hidden it fades out in 0.3 s and `ctx.suspend()`s the shared context, which also freezes SFX; it resumes and fades in on return). hlnative.js also pauses on `appStateChange`. I did not change either. Wake lock: `wlSync()` in main.js requests `navigator.wakeLock` while `view==='game'` and the page is visible, releases otherwise, re-checked on `visibilitychange` and every 2 s; no-op without the API.
- Web-view feel, already handled in ui2.css (found, not changed): overscroll-behavior none, user-select none, no touch callout, transparent tap highlight, `touch-action: manipulation` (no double-tap zoom, no 300 ms delay), inputs and `.invite-url` selectable. Missing and added in src/zz_c10.css: pinch zoom (`touch-action: pan-x pan-y` on html/body; `.sld` keeps `none`), text-size-adjust, image drag, plus a `gesturestart` preventDefault for iOS Safari (main.js).

## Risks / decisions
- Android back on a sheet like "Who goes first" does nothing (by design). A pure web user at home pressing Back leaves the site (expected).
- On the web, a sentinel entry means the first Back after load without any tap leaves the page (Chrome rule), later ones are handled.
- Existing `buzz()` calls and the vibrate shim still fire alongside (e.g. slider release, key taps); haptics add on top. Owner may want to drop `buzz` for taps if it feels too buzzy.
