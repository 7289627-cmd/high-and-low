## Humanising rules (all in botlife.js)
- Think: 1.4 s + exponential (mean ~1.4), x per-bot tempo 0.8-1.3, +0.8-2.2 s when 11-24 left, x1.1/x1.2 in m/l, first move +1.4-3.8 s, 6% chance of a 2.5-5 s pause, obvious final guesses (<=3 left) x0.6 (1 left 0.9-1.8 s). Cap 9 s, never the 40 s clock.
- Asks: botSplit position (still driven by eloSkill) then snapped to a round step (largest of 5,10,25,...,25000 with step*6<=range) 62-80% of the time, a finer step 20%, else raw. Slips (+-8-25% of range) with chance 0.8%+7%*(1-skill). Direction sticks 70%.
- Fill-in shows Picking... for 2-6.5 s (human is held at the start until it is ready; if the human is slower nothing changes).
- Fill-in joins after 5-8 s (was 5.0 s).

- A fill-in that draws a 9 s pause can look slow next to the 40 s clock; fine, still far below it.
- Duel start now waits up to 6.5 s for the fill-in's Ready (secrets screen). Tests with K default 0.2 wait <=1.3 s.
- Not fixed (outside scope): a1 bugs 4-5 (rating dodge etc.). The human's guess answers arrive in <50 ms so bot guess delays in my table include <=0.1 s of measurement noise.
