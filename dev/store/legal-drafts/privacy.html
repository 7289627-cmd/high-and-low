Privacy

Effective EFFECTIVE_DATE

High & Low is a party game. There are no accounts, no email sign-up and no real names. We try to collect as little as we can.

Who we are

High & Low is made by an independent developer (“we”). Questions: see Contact at the bottom.

What the game stores on your device

Your nickname, your bean's look, wins, coins, hats, rating, sound setting, and a random ID for play statistics. The game sends some of these values for online play and statistics as described below.

What we collect

Play statistics. The public iPhone app does not send optional play statistics to our database. The browser game, Android app and some earlier test builds send small usage events to our database, hosted by Supabase. An event has: its name (like “game started”), a random ID made on your device, and a few numbers such as range chosen, number of players and computer players, game length in seconds, win or loss, your rating after a rated game, which power was used, and whether an ad was requested or shown. We also record when an event occurred and whether the game opened inside the app. These events do not include your nickname, room code, email, contacts or precise location. The random ID lets us group events from the same installation.

Online play. To play with others, your nickname, bean look, rating, coin count, random player ID, moves and preset taunts are relayed to other players through Supabase Realtime. The game uses this information to run the room. Service logs may be retained by our hosting providers. The other players see your nickname, so please do not use your real name or personal details.

Servers. Like any server, ours and our providers' servers see your IP address and basic device and browser details when your phone connects. We use this only to run and protect the game. We do not store it ourselves.

Support. If you contact us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use it to respond and look into the issue.

Computer players

Some seats in Play are filled by computer players. They are not real people and no data is collected from them.

Ads (app versions only)

The website shows no ads. The iOS and Android apps can show ads between games through Google AdMob, which is run by Google. Google may collect and use, as described in its own ads policy: device identifiers, IP address (which can give a rough location), information about ads and how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. The first public iPhone release requests limited ads selected using context. Google may use local storage for invalid-traffic detection and fraud prevention when programmatic limited ads are available. The Android app requests non-personalised ads in ad-supported versions. Where a consent message is required, the game checks your choices before requesting ads. When available, you can reopen the consent form from About → Privacy choices. Where optional play statistics are enabled, they can include basic ad events as described above.

What we do not do

  • The game does not require an account, email address or phone number to play.
  • The game does not request access to precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Who handles data

  • Supabase: hosts our statistics database and the live room service.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website, and may log IP addresses to serve pages.
  • Google: AdMob in ad-supported app versions.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We retain play statistics to understand usage and troubleshoot the game. We keep support correspondence to respond and follow up. Device data stays in app or browser storage until cleared. Our service providers retain their records under their own policies. Contact us with questions about retention or deletion.

Delete your data

You can remove local game data by clearing the app's storage on Android or deleting the app on iPhone. This can remove your locally saved rating, coins and hats. Clearing local data does not delete play statistics that were already sent. We may be unable to identify which event records are yours because play statistics use a random installation ID.

To ask about your data or request deletion, use the contact address below.

Your ad choices

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, then turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track. Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising controls Apple's own ads.
  • Android: Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. There you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Menu names differ between phones.

Children

High & Low is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Other countries

Our providers run servers in different countries, so your information may be handled outside the country where you live, including the United States. Where the law asks for it, providers use approved safeguards for these transfers. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the contact below to ask.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes with it. For big changes we will also say so in the game.
