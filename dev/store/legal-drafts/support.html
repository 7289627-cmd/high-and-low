Support

Need a hand with High & Low? Start here.

How do I play?

Everyone hides a secret number. On your turn, ask a player whether their number is higher or lower than a guess.

Use the answers to narrow down their number, then guess it.

The last bean standing wins.

Do I need internet?

Yes. High & Low is played online, so you need a connection.

How do I restore Remove Ads?

Open the game on the new phone while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, and connected to the internet. The game checks your purchase when it starts, so the ads should stop. If they do not, close the game and open it again. You are never charged twice for Remove Ads. Still stuck? Write to us (see below).

How do I report a player?

After a game against one other player, tap the flag on the result screen. That hides the player's name and taunts on your device. For anything more serious, write to us (see below) with the player's nickname, the room code if you have it, roughly when it happened, and what the problem was. We may filter or block names and remove players who break the rules. To stop playing with someone, leave the room.

How do I delete my data?

There are no accounts. Everything on your phone goes if you clear the app's storage or delete the app. That also gives you a new random ID for play statistics. For details and to ask us about data, see the Privacy page.

How do I turn off sounds?

Use the sound button in the game.
