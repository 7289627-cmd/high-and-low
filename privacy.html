Privacy

Effective November 16, 2026

High & Low is a party game. There are no accounts, no email sign-up and no real names. We try to collect as little as we can.

Who we are

High & Low is made by an independent developer (“we”). Questions: see Contact at the bottom.

What stays on your phone

Your nickname, your bean's look, wins, coins, hats, rating, sound setting, whether you bought Remove Ads, and a random ID for play statistics. We do not receive these as a profile. Clearing the app or browser data erases them.

What we collect

Play statistics. When you use the game it sends a small event to our database, hosted by Supabase. An event has: its name (like “game started”), a random ID made on your device, and a few numbers such as range chosen, number of players and computer players, game length in seconds, win or loss, your rating after a rated game, and which power was used. We also mark whether the game opened inside the app. It has no nickname, room code, email, contacts or precise location. The random ID is not tied to who you are.

Online play. To play with others, your nickname, bean look, rating, a random player ID and your moves are sent live to the other players in your room through Supabase Realtime. They are not saved by us. The other players see your nickname, so please do not use your real name or personal details.

Servers. Like any server, ours and our providers' servers see your IP address and basic device and browser details when your phone connects. We use this only to run and protect the game. We do not store it ourselves.

Fonts. The game loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.

Computer players

Some seats in Play are filled by computer players. They are not real people and no data is collected from them.

Ads (app versions only)

The website shows no ads. The iOS and Android apps show ads between games through Google AdMob, which is run by Google. Google may collect and use, as described in its own ads policy: your advertising ID, IP address (which can give a rough location), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information. We ask for non-personalised ads where this is possible. In some regions (for example the EEA and UK) you may be asked for consent first, and you can change it later in the same place. We do not receive this data about you. Ads are not shown if you buy Remove Ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase through the Apple App Store or Google Play. They take your payment, and we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat, a purchase service, to check that Remove Ads is owned and to restore it. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase status and basic device information, and keeps it under its own policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, emails or phone numbers.
  • No selling of personal information.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Who handles data

  • Supabase: hosts our statistics database and the live room service.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website, and may log IP addresses to serve pages.
  • Google: fonts, and AdMob in the apps.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google: purchases in the apps.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Play statistics are deleted after 14 months. Rooms are not saved after they end. Data on your phone stays until you clear it. Ad and purchase providers keep data under their own policies.

Delete your data

Your random ID is made on your device and you cannot see it, so we cannot tell which statistics are yours. The simple way to cut the link is to clear the app's storage (Android: Settings, Apps, High & Low, Storage, Clear storage) or delete the app (iPhone). You get a new random ID, and the old one is never used again. This also erases your rating, coins and hats on that device. Remove Ads can be restored from your store account.

Want to ask about your data, or for us to delete what we can? Write to us (see Contact). We will answer within a reasonable time.

Your ad choices

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking, then turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track. Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising controls Apple's own ads.
  • Android: Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. There you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation. Menu names differ between phones.

Children

High & Low is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Other countries

Our providers run servers in different countries, so your information may be handled outside the country where you live, including the United States. Where the law asks for it, providers use approved safeguards for these transfers. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your data, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Use the contact below to ask.

Changes

If we change this page, the date at the top changes with it. For big changes we will also say so in the game.
