Privacy

Last updated November 12, 2026

High & Low is a small party game. It is built to work without an account and to collect as little as possible.

What the game stores on your device

Your nickname, your bean's look, your wins, coins and unlocked hats, your sound setting, and a random ID used for play statistics. This stays in your browser or app storage. Clearing your browser or app data removes it.

What is sent while you play

To run a game, your nickname, your bean's look and your moves are sent to the other players in your room through our realtime provider, Supabase. Your secret number stays on your device until the game needs to reveal it. Game rooms are not saved after they end.

Play statistics

We record simple events so we can see how the game is used: that the game was opened, that a game started or ended, how long it lasted, how many players and bots were in it, and whether a power was used. Each event carries the random ID from your device. Events do not include your nickname, room code, anything you type, your precise location or your contacts.

Bots

When you tap Play, empty seats are filled by computer players.

Ads and purchases

The website shows no ads. App versions may show ads between games and offer a one-time purchase that removes them. If ads are shown, the ad provider may collect device information as described in its own policy, and this page will name that provider. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Who we share with

Supabase hosts the realtime service and the statistics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses to deliver the page. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can play without giving a name. You can delete everything stored on your device by clearing the site or app data. To ask about data or request deletion of statistics tied to your device ID, use the contact address on the game's store page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
